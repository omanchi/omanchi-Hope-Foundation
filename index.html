About the Founder

Samuel is a passionate humanitarian and visionary dedicated to changing the lives of vulnerable children. After witnessing the struggles of homeless and abandoned kids, he established the Omanchi Hope Foundation to provide them with shelter, education, and a second chance at life.

Through his leadership, dozens of children have been rescued from the streets and placed in a safe, loving environment. Samuel believes every child deserves dignity, opportunity, and hope.

Your support helps turn this vision into reality.
